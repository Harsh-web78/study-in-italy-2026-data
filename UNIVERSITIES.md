## 3. University of Turin (UniTo)

**Official name:** Università degli Studi di Torino  
**Location:** Turin, Piedmont  
**Official website:** https://en.unito.it/

### 2026/27 application references

European Dreams research identified:

- Postgraduate application period: 25 November 2025 – 29 January 2026
- Undergraduate / 5–6 year programmes: 19 February – 15 April 2026
- Universitaly visa pre-enrolment reference deadline: 15 July 2026

### Tuition reference

The University of Turin uses a multi-instalment contribution mechanism based on the applicable fee band.

A published reference identified:

- First instalment: €156
- Additional instalments vary according to the applicable band and conditions.
