## University-Level Sources

University-specific information should be verified directly against the relevant university's official website.

Examples include:

- University of Milan
- University of Genoa
- University of Turin
- Sapienza University of Rome
- University of Padua
- University of Bologna
- University of Bergamo
- University of Verona
- University of Camerino
- University of Naples Federico II

University-level sources should be preferred for:

- Programme availability
- Tuition fees
- Application deadlines
- Admission requirements
- Course language
- Scholarships
- Application procedures

---

## Visa & Consular Sources

Visa requirements should be verified through the relevant Italian Embassy or Consulate and the official visa information applicable to the applicant's jurisdiction.

Official information may include:

- Student visa requirements
- Financial requirements
- Required documents
- Appointment procedures
- Universitaly requirements
- Local consular instructions

---

## Source Priority

When sources conflict, use the following priority:

1. Italian government / ministry source
2. Universitaly
3. Italian Embassy / Consulate
4. Official university website
5. Official scholarship provider
6. European Commission / European Education Area
7. Established secondary education sources

---

## Verification Principles

This repository does not treat a secondary website as the final authority for:

- visa rules
- immigration requirements
- university deadlines
- tuition fees
- scholarship eligibility
- programme admission requirements

Information should be checked against the applicable primary source before an application or financial decision.
