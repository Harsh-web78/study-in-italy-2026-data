## 3. Universitaly

Universitaly is the official portal used for international-student procedures and visa-related pre-enrolment.

Official source:

https://www.universitaly.it/it/studenti-stranieri

Key points:

- International students may need to complete pre-enrolment through Universitaly.
- University validation does not guarantee visa issuance.
- Final visa decisions are made by the competent Italian diplomatic/consular authority.
- Applicants should follow the applicable consular and university deadlines.

---

## 4. Application Journey

Typical sequence:

1. Research universities.
2. Select programmes.
3. Check admission requirements.
4. Check tuition and funding options.
5. Prepare documents.
6. Submit university application.
7. Complete Universitaly pre-enrolment where required.
8. Prepare the student-visa application.
9. Follow the applicable consular procedure.
10. Complete post-arrival requirements.

The exact process can vary by university, programme and applicant category.

## 6. Data Verification

Every university-specific entry should be verified against the official university source.

Every visa-related entry should be verified against:

- Universitaly
- Italian Embassy / Consulate
- applicable official visa instructions

Every scholarship entry should be verified against the official scholarship provider.

---

## 7. Data Limitations

This dataset should not be interpreted as a guarantee of:

- admission
- scholarship selection
- visa approval
- fixed tuition
- fixed living costs

Official requirements and deadlines can change.
